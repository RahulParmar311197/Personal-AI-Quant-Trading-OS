# AI Rules

1. AI/ML outputs are probabilistic evidence, not orders.
2. Models require time-aware train/validation/test separation.
3. Feature timestamps must be point-in-time correct.
4. Training and inference datasets must be versioned.
5. Leakage checks are mandatory before model evaluation.
6. Model versions, features, metrics and experiments must be reproducible.
7. Failed or unavailable models must have explicit fallback behavior.
8. No model accuracy or profitability claim is accepted without measured validation evidence.
9. AI cannot bypass Decision or Risk layers.
10. Production model promotion requires documented acceptance criteria.
