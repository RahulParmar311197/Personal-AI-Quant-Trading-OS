# Product Vision

## Mission
Build a personal, research-first quantitative trading operating system that converts market data into explainable opportunities while enforcing deterministic risk controls.

## V1
NSE-first, NIFTY/BANKNIFTY-first research and paper-trading system with multi-timeframe analysis, technical/price-action/market-structure/SMC/ICT features, options/context data, backtesting, validation, decisioning, risk and auditability.

## Design principles
- Evidence over prediction claims.
- Modular domain boundaries.
- Deterministic risk independent of strategy and AI.
- Reproducible, point-in-time-correct research.
- Paper trading before controlled live execution.
- Personal use first; SaaS extensibility later.

## Explicit non-goal
V1 does not guarantee profitability and does not authorize unattended real-money trading.
